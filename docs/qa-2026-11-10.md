## Fixed
1. Preet could tell a customer the old work date after the contractor moved the job (it read the quote's date). Now it reports the job's real day (`work_day`, and the quote's `work_date` follows the job).
2. Five internal helper functions (`invoice_json`, `assistant_job_label`, `crew_job_row`, `crew_pick_job`, `member_contractor`) could be called by any signed-in user; `invoice_json` could show any invoice if its ID was known. Now only other database functions can use them.
3. Pinned `search_path` on 11 small helper functions (advisor warning).
4. Pros app: password-reset links now open a "Set a new password" screen; admins who land in Pros are sent to the admin dashboard (in GitHub/Vercel; Hostinger copy waits for re-sign-in).

## Not bugs, but blocking launch
- Customers can't pay an invoice yet: no Interac email and no Square card link are set (the invoice shows neither).
- No invoice WhatsApp message (template `invoice_ready` not approved/enabled).
- Emails (sign-in links, invites) fail: `SMTP_PASSWORD` secret not set.
- Google sign-in not switched on.
- Supabase "leaked password protection" is off (Auth → Settings) — recommended on.
