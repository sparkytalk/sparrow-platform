Questions

Good to know

Is there a contract?

No. You pay month to month and can cancel anytime from the app. You keep access until the end of the month you've paid for.

Does Sparrow take a cut of my jobs?

No. Your customers pay you directly by e-Transfer, cheque, cash or card. Sparrow never touches the money and charges no commission.

How many crew members can I add?

The $11.99 crew add-on covers up to 10 people. If you have a bigger team, write to us at info@sparrowlabs.ca.

What about GST and PST?

Your subscription is charged in Canadian dollars with 5% GST and 7% BC PST added. On your own quotes and invoices, the app adds GST for you and shows your GST number if you have one.

What happens when my trial ends?

Choose a plan to keep going. If you don't, your account goes read-only: you can still see and download your jobs and customers, but you can't create new ones until you pick a plan.

Do my crew need to pay or download anything?

No. Crew are covered by your add-on and use the app in their phone's browser. They can add it to their home screen like any app.

Who owns my data?

You do. Your jobs, customers and invoices are yours, stored securely, and you can ask us for a full export at any time.
