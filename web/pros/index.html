What it does

From the first call to the paid invoice.

No spreadsheets, no paper quote books, no chasing texts. Every job lives in one place you can open in the truck.

Quotes customers approve online

Write a quote on your phone and send it by text, email or WhatsApp. Your customer opens it and taps Approve. GST is added for you.

Invoices with your branding

Your logo, your colours and how to pay you on every invoice. Customers pay you directly. Sparrow takes no commission.

Jobs, customers and calendar

Every customer, address and job in one list, with today's work at the top. Photos and notes saved on each job.

Your crew, organised

Your crew gets their own app: the jobs you give them, clock in and out, daily reports and photos. Timesheets are ready for payroll.

Questions

Good to know

Is it really free?

Yes. Sparrow Pros is free while we open it up to our first businesses. No card needed. We'll tell you well ahead of time before any paid plans start, and you choose whether to stay.

Why do you approve accounts?

We're opening Sparrow Pros to a small group of real businesses first, so we can help each one get set up properly and fix anything that gets in their way.

Does Sparrow take a cut of my jobs?

No. Your customers pay you directly by e-Transfer, cheque, cash or card. Sparrow never touches the money.

Do my crew need to download anything?

No. Crew open the app in their phone's browser from the invite you send them, and can add it to their home screen like any app.

Who owns my data?

You do. Your jobs, customers and invoices are yours, stored securely, and you can ask us for a full copy at any time.
